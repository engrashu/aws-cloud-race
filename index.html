AWS Cloud Race

AWS Cloud Practitioner Exam Prep Game — 55 Questions

How to Play

  1. You will race through 55 AWS questions covering 6 weeks of content.
  2. Each question has 4 options — pick the correct answer.
  3. A correct answer moves you forward on the track. A wrong answer marks it red.
  4. After each answer, read the explanation to learn why it's correct.
  5. At the end, you get your score and grade.

Topics covered: AWS Global Infrastructure, Compute, Storage, Databases, Networking, Security, IAM, Well-Architected Framework, Pricing, Support, and more.
